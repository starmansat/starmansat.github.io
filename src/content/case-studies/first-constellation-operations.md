## The situation

An imaging startup has one satellite on orbit and four more on the launch manifest. Its operations run on the founders' scripts and memory: two engineers take every pass, and procedures exist mostly as chat messages.

## What we would do

1. **Assess.** Shadow a week of operations, inventory every manual step, and rank them by frequency and risk.
2. **Capture.** Turn the routine passes and the known anomalies into tested procedures, run against the satellite simulator.
3. **Automate.** Script the routine passes end to end, with operators supervising and alerts routed to the person on call.
4. **Rehearse.** Run launch and early-operations rehearsals for the next satellites with the whole team.

## What changes

Routine passes run without an operator at the console. The team spends its time on the work that needs people: anomalies, tasking and improvements. New satellites join the fleet through a rehearsed process instead of a scramble.
